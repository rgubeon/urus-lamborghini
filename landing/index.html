FAQ

Which commercial airport is nearest to the venue?

Seville Airport (SVQ – Aeropuerto de Sevilla-San Pablo) is the main international airport serving the area and is located approximately 12 km from your hotel.

What is the average temperature in Seville between late February and early March?

Seville enjoys mild and pleasant spring-like weather during late February and early March, with average daytime temperatures around 19°C and nighttime temperatures around 8°C. The city typically experiences plenty of sunshine, offering comfortable conditions for outdoor activities.

Where is the Seville Circuit located?

The Seville Circuit, officially known as Circuito de Sevilla, is located in Carmona, approximately 25 km east of Seville city centre.

How long is the transfer from the hotel to the Circuito de Sevilla?

The transfer from Hotel to the Circuito de Sevilla takes approximately 25–30 minutes via our dedicated event shuttles, depending on traffic conditions.

What is the dress code for the event?

Smart casual attire is recommended for dinners and evening events. For the driving sessions at the Circuito de Sevilla, comfortable clothing and flat shoes.

Are helmets and racing gear provided at the circuit?

Yes, all necessary safety equipment, including helmets and balaclavas, will be provided on-site by our team at the circuit.

Will dietary restrictions and allergies be accommodated?

Yes, all meals are curated to accommodate specific dietary requirements. Please make sure to indicate any allergies, intolerances, or preferences during the registration process so our catering team can prepare accordingly.
