Visa
Citizens from non-EU countries may enter Spain with a passport valid for at least three months after the planned date of departure from the Schengen Area. If you need a visa to enter Spain, please contact your local Spanish embassy. You can find important information on the entry requirements for Spain here.

Please note that applying for a visa is an individual process and we cannot apply on your behalf. Let us know as soon as possible if you need a visa for your trip to Spain: our concierge team will be pleased to support you by providing an invitation letter when needed.

Important: please verify that your passport expires in at least six months' time.
